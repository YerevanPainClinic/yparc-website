Nerve pain

Neuropathic pain

Neuropathic pain is linked to a lesion or disease of the nervous system that carries sensation. It describes a pain mechanism rather than one diagnosis.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Burning or electric pain
  • Tingling or numbness
  • Pain caused by light touch
  • Pain spreading along a nerve distribution
02

How it is assessed

Assessment considers sensation, strength, pain distribution and injury or illness history. Nerve conduction testing or further investigations may sometimes be needed.

03

Approaches to care

Care may include management of the cause, medication suited to neuropathic pain, rehabilitation and selected procedures. Burning pain alone does not confirm the diagnosis.

When to seek urgent help

Sudden or rapidly increasing weakness, abrupt loss of sensation or new bladder or bowel problems need immediate assessment.
