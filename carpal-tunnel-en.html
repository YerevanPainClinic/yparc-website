Pain conditions / Nerve pain

Nerve pain

Carpal tunnel syndrome

Compression of the median nerve in the wrist tunnel can cause hand pain and altered sensation.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Tingling in the thumb, index and middle fingers
  • Night numbness or weak grip
02

How it is assessed

Sensation and muscle strength are checked. Nerve conduction studies may clarify the severity of compression.

03

Approaches to care

Depending on severity, care may include wrist positioning, a night splint and other treatments. Persistent weakness needs assessment.

When to seek urgent help

Sudden or rapidly increasing weakness, abrupt loss of sensation or new bladder or bowel problems need immediate assessment.
