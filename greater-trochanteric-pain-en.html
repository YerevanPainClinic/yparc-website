Pain conditions / Hip, knee and foot

Hip, knee and foot

Greater trochanteric pain syndrome

This is an umbrella term for outer hip pain involving gluteal tendons and nearby soft tissues. It is not always simply bursitis.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Local outer hip tenderness
  • Pain lying on the side or climbing stairs
02

How it is assessed

Examination distinguishes tendon pain from hip joint pain and pain referred from the lower back.

03

Approaches to care

Care includes reducing pressure and excessive load, suitable movement and strengthening.

When to seek urgent help

Inability to bear weight after injury, a hot red joint with fever, or a suddenly cold pale foot need urgent assessment.
