Sciatica

Sciatica is pain spreading down the leg due to irritation of the sciatic nerve or its nerve roots, often from a lower back problem.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain from the buttock down the leg
  • Tingling, numbness or weakness
02

How it is assessed

Examination checks leg strength, sensation and reflexes. Imaging is selected according to findings and progress.

03

Approaches to care

Care depends on severity and may include adjusted activity, rehabilitation and pain management. Increasing weakness needs prompt assessment.

When to seek urgent help

New bladder or bowel problems, numbness around the saddle area, rapidly increasing weakness or pain after major trauma need immediate assessment.
