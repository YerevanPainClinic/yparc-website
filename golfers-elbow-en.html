Shoulder, elbow and hand

Golfer’s elbow

Tendon pain on the inner elbow is often linked to repeated gripping or wrist flexion.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Inner elbow pain and tenderness
  • Pain with gripping or wrist flexion
02

How it is assessed

Examination distinguishes a tendon problem from ulnar nerve involvement, especially if fingers are numb.

03

Approaches to care

Load modification is combined with progressive strengthening.

When to seek urgent help

Major injury, a deformed joint, sudden loss of arm strength, or a hot red joint with fever need prompt medical assessment.
