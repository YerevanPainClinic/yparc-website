Pain conditions / Nerve pain

Nerve pain

Intercostal neuralgia

Pain involving nerves between the ribs may spread around the chest or abdominal wall. Chest pain cannot be assumed to be nerve pain from symptoms alone.

01

What you may feel

  • Band-like burning or stabbing
  • Pain with touch or body movement
02

How it is assessed

Assessment first considers cardiac, lung and other internal causes, then nerve distribution and previous surgery or rash.

03

Approaches to care

Confirmed nerve pain is treated according to its cause. New chest pain with breathlessness or cold sweating needs emergency care.

When to seek urgent help

Sudden or rapidly increasing weakness, abrupt loss of sensation or new bladder or bowel problems need immediate assessment.
