Pain conditions / Nerve pain

Nerve pain

Pudendal neuralgia

Pain involving the pudendal nerve may affect the perineum, genital or anal area. Similar pain also has other causes.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Burning or stabbing perineal pain
  • Discomfort worsened by sitting
02

How it is assessed

Assessment considers sitting, pelvic floor function and other urological or gynaecological causes.

03

Approaches to care

Care may include sitting adjustments, appropriate pelvic floor rehabilitation and specialist pain treatment.

When to seek urgent help

Sudden or rapidly increasing weakness, abrupt loss of sensation or new bladder or bowel problems need immediate assessment.
