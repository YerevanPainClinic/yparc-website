Pain conditions / Shoulder, elbow and hand

Shoulder, elbow and hand

Rotator cuff pain

The rotator cuff is a group of muscles and tendons that move and stabilise the shoulder. Pain may involve tendon irritation or a tear.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain when lifting the arm
  • Night pain or reduced strength
02

How it is assessed

Active and passive movement and strength are checked. Ultrasound or MRI may help when the result would change care.

03

Approaches to care

Care often centres on load adjustment and gradual strengthening. Marked weakness after an injury needs assessment.

When to seek urgent help

Major injury, a deformed joint, sudden loss of arm strength, or a hot red joint with fever need prompt medical assessment.
