After injury or surgery

Persistent post-surgical pain

Pain after surgery usually decreases as healing progresses. Pain persisting beyond the expected recovery period needs reassessment.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain that is not improving or newly worsening
  • Scar sensitivity or limited function
02

How it is assessed

The operation and recovery are reviewed. Infection, structural complications and persistence of the original problem must first be considered.

03

Approaches to care

Care is adapted to the identified cause in collaboration with the surgical and rehabilitation teams.

When to seek urgent help

Wound redness or discharge, fever, rapidly worsening pain or new weakness need prompt assessment. Sudden breathlessness or chest pain requires emergency care.
