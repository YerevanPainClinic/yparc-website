Pain conditions / Nerve pain

Nerve pain

Morton’s neuroma

Irritation of a nerve between the toes often feels like burning pain or a pebble in the shoe.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Burning pain in the forefoot
  • Tingling or numbness in the toes
02

How it is assessed

Assessment checks tenderness between the toes and the effect of footwear. Imaging may help when the diagnosis is uncertain.

03

Approaches to care

Care may include footwear with a wider toe box, pressure reduction and further treatment when needed.

When to seek urgent help

Sudden or rapidly increasing weakness, abrupt loss of sensation or new bladder or bowel problems need immediate assessment.
