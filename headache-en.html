Head and face

Headache

Headache is not a single diagnosis. Assessment distinguishes migraine, tension-type headache, neck-related or nerve pain, and other causes.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pressing or throbbing pain
  • Pain on one or both sides
  • Nausea or sensitivity to light or sound
  • A link with neck movement or pain at the back of the head
02

How it is assessed

Assessment reviews attack duration and frequency, associated symptoms and medication use. A headache diary can help. Imaging is not necessary in every case.

03

Approaches to care

Treatment depends on the headache type. Options may include attack management, prevention, sleep and routine adjustments, and referral to an appropriate specialist.

Good to know

Frequent painkiller use can sometimes maintain or worsen headaches. Discuss medication changes with a clinician.

When to seek urgent help

A sudden unusually severe headache, new speech or vision problems, weakness, altered consciousness, or a stiff neck with fever need emergency care.
