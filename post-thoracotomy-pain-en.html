After injury or surgery

Pain after thoracic surgery

Pain after thoracotomy or other thoracic surgery may involve intercostal nerves and the scar.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Burning or pain along the ribs
  • Pain with deep breathing or movement
02

How it is assessed

New breathlessness, fever or a change in chest pain must be assessed before attributing it to a scar.

03

Approaches to care

Care may combine nerve pain treatment with breathing and movement rehabilitation.

When to seek urgent help

Wound redness or discharge, fever, rapidly worsening pain or new weakness need prompt assessment. Sudden breathlessness or chest pain requires emergency care.
