Head and face

Glossopharyngeal neuralgia

This is an uncommon nerve pain affecting the throat, back of the tongue or ear, often in brief attacks.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Attacks triggered by swallowing or talking
  • Pain spreading from the throat towards the ear
02

How it is assessed

Local ENT and other causes must be excluded. An attack with fainting needs urgent assessment.

03

Approaches to care

Treatment is planned after specialist assessment using an approach suited to nerve pain.

When to seek urgent help

A sudden unusually severe headache, new speech or vision problems, weakness, altered consciousness, or a stiff neck with fever need emergency care.
