Pain conditions / Back and neck

Back and neck

Neck pain

Neck pain may arise from muscles, joints, discs or nerves. It sometimes spreads to the shoulder, shoulder blade or arm.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Local pain or stiffness
  • Difficulty turning the head
  • Pain spreading to the shoulder or arm
  • Arm numbness, tingling or weakness
02

How it is assessed

Assessment considers onset, injury history, movement and neurological signs. Test results are interpreted alongside symptoms and examination findings.

03

Approaches to care

Depending on the cause, care may involve pain management, adjustments to movement and workload, and personalised rehabilitation. Procedures are considered after assessment.

When to seek urgent help

New bladder or bowel problems, numbness around the saddle area, rapidly increasing weakness or pain after major trauma need immediate assessment.
