Nerve pain

Complex regional pain syndrome (CRPS)

CRPS is a persistent limb pain condition, often after injury, with changes in sensation, movement and skin.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Marked pain and touch sensitivity
  • Swelling, colour or temperature changes
02

How it is assessed

Diagnosis relies on a combination of findings and exclusion of other causes, including infection and vascular problems.

03

Approaches to care

Early assessment and individual rehabilitation matter. Care may combine gradual movement, pain management and desensitisation.

When to seek urgent help

Sudden or rapidly increasing weakness, abrupt loss of sensation or new bladder or bowel problems need immediate assessment.
