Hip, knee and foot

Heel pain

Heel pain can arise from the plantar fascia, Achilles tendon, fat pad, nerves or bone. Its precise location matters.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain under the heel with first steps
  • Pain at the back of the heel with activity
  • Pain after walking or standing
  • Burning, numbness or tingling
02

How it is assessed

Examination distinguishes fascia, tendon, nerve and injury-related causes. X-rays, ultrasound or MRI are used selectively.

03

Approaches to care

Care is tailored to the cause through activity, footwear, exercise and pain management. Similar heel pain does not always need the same treatment.

Good to know

A heel spur may be present without pain. An X-ray finding alone does not establish the cause of pain.

When to seek urgent help

Inability to bear weight after injury, a hot red joint with fever, or a suddenly cold pale foot need urgent assessment.
