About YPARC

Learn about our multidisciplinary, patient-centred approach to pain care and rehabilitation.

Understanding the problem

Pain is personal and can be influenced by physical, psychological and social factors. A clinical assessment considers your symptoms, health, goals and daily activities before recommendations are made.

How we can help

Care may include clear education, an individual activity plan, medication review, rehabilitation or an image-guided procedure when appropriate. Decisions are shared, and benefits, limitations and alternatives are discussed.

When to seek urgent help

Seek urgent medical care for severe symptoms after major trauma, new marked weakness, loss of bladder or bowel control, chest pain, breathing difficulty, confusion, fever with severe illness, or a sudden unusually severe headache.

Next step

If pain persists, worsens or limits sleep, movement or daily life, arrange an assessment. This information is educational and does not replace personalised medical advice.
