Shoulder, elbow and hand

Shoulder pain

Shoulder pain may involve tendons, bursae, joints, restricted movement or pain referred from the neck. The source guides treatment.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain when raising the arm
  • Difficulty dressing or reaching behind the back
  • Night pain or difficulty lying on the painful side
  • Weakness or restricted movement
02

How it is assessed

Examination compares active and passive movement, strength and possible neck involvement. Ultrasound, X-rays or MRI are selected for the suspected cause.

03

Approaches to care

Options include activity adjustment, pain management and progressive exercise. Injections or surgical assessment are considered in selected cases.

When to seek urgent help

Major injury, a deformed joint, sudden loss of arm strength, or a hot red joint with fever need prompt medical assessment.
