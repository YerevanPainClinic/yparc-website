Hip, knee and foot

Knee pain

Knee pain may arise from joints, menisci, ligaments, tendons or overload. Pain severity does not always match X-ray or MRI findings.

On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain with walking or stairs
  • Stiffness or swelling
  • Difficulty rising from a chair
  • Instability or a locked knee
02

How it is assessed

Assessment considers injury mechanism, walking, swelling, range of motion and stability. Tests are selected according to the examination.

03

Approaches to care

Care may include activity modification, rebuilding strength and movement, and individual pain management. Procedures depend on the diagnosis.

When to seek urgent help

Inability to bear weight after injury, a hot red joint with fever, or a suddenly cold pale foot need urgent assessment.
