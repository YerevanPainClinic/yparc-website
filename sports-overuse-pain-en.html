Pain conditions / Hip, knee and foot

Hip, knee and foot

Sports overuse pain

When training load rises faster than tissues can adapt, pain may develop in tendons, muscles or bones.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Recurrent pain during or after training
  • Slower recovery or reduced performance
02

How it is assessed

Assessment reviews load changes, recovery, technique and local pain. Focal bone pain may require further investigation.

03

Approaches to care

Return to sport is staged, maintaining tolerable activity and monitoring the pain response.

When to seek urgent help

Inability to bear weight after injury, a hot red joint with fever, or a suddenly cold pale foot need urgent assessment.
