Pain conditions / Nerve pain

Nerve pain

Nerve entrapment pain

Local pressure on a nerve may cause pain, numbness or weakness in its distribution. Carpal and cubital tunnel syndromes are specific examples.

01

What you may feel

  • Tingling worsened by position or movement
  • Changes in sensation or strength along a nerve
02

How it is assessed

Assessment locates the compression and distinguishes it from a nerve root problem or more widespread neuropathy.

03

Approaches to care

Care depends on location and severity, from reducing pressure to specialist treatment.

When to seek urgent help

Sudden or rapidly increasing weakness, abrupt loss of sensation or new bladder or bowel problems need immediate assessment.
