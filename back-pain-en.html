Pain conditions / Back and neck

Back and neck

Back and lower back pain

Lower back pain may involve muscles, joints, discs or nerve roots. It may stay in the back or spread to the buttock and leg.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain when bending or straightening
  • Pain with prolonged sitting or standing
  • Pain spreading down the leg
  • Numbness, tingling or a change in strength
02

How it is assessed

Assessment includes the pain history, movement and walking, and strength, sensation and reflexes when needed. MRI or X-rays are selected according to clinical findings.

03

Approaches to care

Care may include pain management, personalised exercise and a gradual return to activity. Targeted procedures are considered only when clinically appropriate.

Good to know

MRI changes do not always explain the pain or mean that surgery is needed.

When to seek urgent help

New bladder or bowel problems, numbness around the saddle area, rapidly increasing weakness or pain after major trauma need immediate assessment.
