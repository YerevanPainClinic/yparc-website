Pain conditions / Hip, knee and foot

Hip, knee and foot

Hip pain

Pain in the groin, side of the hip or buttock may come from the joint, tendons, soft tissues or referred lower back pain.

Book a visit
On this pageWhat you may feelHow it is assessedApproaches to careWhen to seek urgent help
01

What you may feel

  • Pain with walking or stairs
  • Groin or outer-thigh pain
  • Pain when lying on the side
  • Reduced movement or limping
02

How it is assessed

Assessment reviews hip movement, gait, tendon tenderness and possible lower back involvement. Clinical findings determine which tests are needed.

03

Approaches to care

Care includes suitable activity adjustment, exercise and pain management. Lateral hip pain and problems inside the joint may need different approaches.

When to seek urgent help

Inability to bear weight after injury, a hot red joint with fever, or a suddenly cold pale foot need urgent assessment.
